ST131Finder

Assign clade and subclade (A / B0 / B1 / C0 / C1-M27 / C1-nM27 / C2) to Escherichia coli ST131 assemblies by in silico PCR. Each run also reports MLST, O:H serotype, gyrA/parC QRDR substitutions and acquired resistance genes including ESBLs.

Implements the primer set in Table 1 and the interpretation matrix in Table 2 of Matsumura Y, Pitout JDD, Peirano G, et al. Rapid identification of different Escherichia coli sequence type 131 clades. Antimicrob Agents Chemother 2017;61:e00179-17.

Data handling

Sequences are processed entirely in your browser and never leave your machine, so unpublished assemblies can be analysed as they are.

How calls are made, and what the tool cannot do

  • Allele-specific primers. The published primers carry deliberate internal mismatches, so an exact match is the wrong test. A band is called when the 3′-terminal base matches, no more than two mismatches occur anywhere in the primer, and the product is within 25 bp of the expected size. Open “Show primer alignments” on any positive assay to see the bases the call rests on.
  • Draft assemblies. An amplicon that straddles a contig boundary is scored negative. When M27PP1 is negative but the aer SNP assay is positive, the isolate is still reported as C1-M27 and a warning explains why.
  • Order of results. Browsers hand over multi-file selections in an unpredictable order, so files are sorted by name (numeric-aware) before analysis. The most recently analysed isolate appears at the top; run numbers on the cards follow the order of analysis.
  • fimH and subclone assays. fimH30, 22, 27, 35 and 41, the H30Rx ybbW SNP, rfb O25b/O16 and fliC H4/H5 are called with the primers of Johnson et al. 2022. Clade C isolates normally carry fimH30, but alternative alleles occur, so the two calls are reported independently and a warning appears when they disagree.
  • Not a substitute for PCR or WGS phylogeny. These are in silico calls. For epidemiological work, confirm clade assignment by core-genome phylogeny.
  • Reference data. MLST uses the PubMLST Escherichia coli #1 (Achtman) scheme; resistance genes use ResFinder; O and H antigen alleles come from the ECTyper set. Databases were retrieved in August 2026.
